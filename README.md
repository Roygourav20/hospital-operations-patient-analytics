# Hospital Operations & Patient Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes hospital operational data using **MySQL** to understand patient behavior, appointment patterns, doctor workload, treatment trends, and revenue performance.

The analysis combines five relational datasets — patients, doctors, appointments, treatments, and billing — to generate actionable insights that can support hospital operational and patient-management decisions.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The project focuses on answering key business questions such as:

* What is the demographic distribution of hospital patients?
* Which medical specializations have the highest appointment demand?
* Which doctors handle the highest number of appointments?
* What is the appointment status distribution?
* Which patients have high no-show/cancellation rates?
* What are the monthly appointment and revenue trends?
* Which patients generate the highest revenue?
* Which patients are frequent, high-value, or potentially at-risk?
* How frequently do patients return for appointments?

# 📊 Key Findings

### Appointment Performance

* The dataset contains **200 appointments**.
* **51.5%** of appointments were either cancelled or marked as no-show.
* **20 patients** had more than three appointments and a missed-appointment rate above 40%.
* **Pediatrics** had the highest appointment volume with **98 appointments**.
* **Tuesday and Wednesday** were the busiest appointment days, with **37 appointments each**.
* **April 2023** recorded the highest monthly appointment volume with **25 appointments**.

### Doctor Performance

* The dataset contains **10 doctors**.
* **Sarah Taylor** handled the highest number of appointments with **29 appointments**.
* Doctor workload was analyzed using appointment volume and ranking techniques.

### Revenue Performance

* Total paid revenue generated was **₹173,424.90**.
* **P012** was the highest revenue-generating patient with paid spending of **₹15,629.88**.
* **June 2023** generated the highest monthly paid revenue of **₹30,474.04**.

### Treatment Analysis

* **Chemotherapy** was the most frequently recorded treatment with **49 treatments**.
* Average treatment cost was **₹2,756.25**.

# 💡 Business Insights & Recommendations

Based on the analysis, several operational opportunities can be identified:

### 1. Reduce missed appointments

With **51.5% of appointments classified as cancelled or no-show**, appointment adherence represents an important operational area.

**Recommendation:**
Implement automated appointment reminders, follow-up calls, and targeted communication for patients with repeated missed appointments.

### 2. Monitor high-demand specializations

Pediatrics recorded the highest appointment volume with **98 appointments**.

**Recommendation:**
Hospital management can monitor staffing levels and appointment capacity in high-demand specializations to reduce potential workload bottlenecks.

### 3. Monitor doctor workload

Sarah Taylor handled the highest number of appointments.

**Recommendation:**
Use doctor workload metrics to identify potential capacity issues and support better appointment allocation.

### 4. Identify high-value patients

RFM segmentation and revenue analysis can identify patients who contribute significant revenue or visit frequently.

**Recommendation:**
Use patient segmentation to support personalized follow-ups, retention strategies, and targeted patient engagement.

### 5. Monitor revenue trends

June 2023 recorded the highest monthly paid revenue.

**Recommendation:**
Analyze monthly revenue fluctuations alongside appointment volume and treatment demand to understand the drivers behind revenue changes.
